01 — About

Not just an idea.
Build it.

The Venture Lab is a student-led community for young people who want to learn, build and create alongside others.

Share ideas, meet other builders, work on projects, learn new skills and turn ideas into something real.

Our work happens remotely, so members can participate from wherever they are.

13–19
The Venture Lab is exclusively for people aged 13 to 19.

Applications are reviewed before members are invited into the community.
